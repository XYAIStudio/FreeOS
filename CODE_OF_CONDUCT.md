# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

Participants must communicate professionally, welcome good-faith questions, respect privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers rather than in a public thread. Reports will be handled with appropriate confidentiality and without retaliation.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
